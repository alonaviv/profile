Once a year, upload the school's teachers file: everyone in it is a teacher who may have an account. Every teacher is matched by ת.ז.: new teachers get a record so they can register (registration asks for their ת.ז.), and active teachers who aren't in the file are soft-deleted and can no longer sign in (their classes and evaluations stay). Superusers are never touched. Names already in the system are never changed.

First submit without "Apply" to preview. To apply, select the file again, tick "Apply" and submit.
