Upload the two roster files from the Ministry of Education (א'-ו' and ז'-יב''), as the office exports them, at the start of the school year and whenever the roster changes. Every child is matched by ת.ז.: their house and class are updated, new children are created, and active students who aren't in the files are soft-deleted (their evaluations stay). Names already in the system are never changed.

First submit without "Apply" to preview. To apply, select the files again, tick "Apply" and submit.
